Frequently asked

What is a 1031 Exchange?

A 1031 Exchange allows you to defer capital gain taxes when swapping one real estate investment property for another. When you sell your investment property, the proceeds must be held in escrow by a third party and then used to purchase a new property. This process allows investors to maximize their return on investment and defer taxes.

What are the advantages of a 1031 Exchange?

The main advantage is postponing the payment of capital gain taxes and the recapture of depreciation when selling and reinvesting in like-kind investment properties. That gives real estate investors and taxpayers more capital to invest, more cash flow from income-producing properties, and the ability to rebalance and grow commercial real estate portfolios.
