Pennsylvania closing essentials

The line items that define a PA closing.

PA-specific taxes, premium rates, and endorsements that should be reviewed at file open.

Closing format

PA is a title state. The title agency runs the closing; attorney involvement is optional, though common on commercial deals.

Realty Transfer Tax

1% state + 1% local by default, traditionally split between buyer and seller. Philadelphia carries a higher local rate of 3.278%, bringing total RTT inside the city to 4.278%.

Filed premium rates

PA title insurance rates are filed with the PA Insurance Department. Basic rate vs. reissue rate matters — reissue can save the buyer hundreds to thousands on a refinance or recent re-sale.

ALTA endorsements

Common PA lender requirements include ALTA 9 (restrictions/encroachments), ALTA 8.1 (environmental), and others. We handle the endorsement stack in-house.
