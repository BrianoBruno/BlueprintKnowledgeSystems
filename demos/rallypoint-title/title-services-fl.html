Florida closing essentials

The line items that trip up a FL closing.

FL-specific taxes, exemptions, and withholdings that should be caught at file open.

Closing format

FL is a title state. A title agent or attorney can close the deal. We close — coordinating with any attorney involved.

Documentary Stamp Tax — Deed

$0.70 per $100 of consideration statewide. Miami-Dade has its own rate structure on single-family transactions. Seller-paid by convention, negotiable.

Documentary Stamp Tax — Mortgage

$0.35 per $100 of the loan amount, paid on financed transactions. Borrower-paid; included in the closing cost estimate.

Intangible Tax

2 mills (0.2%) on the loan amount, paid at closing on financed transactions. Material on jumbos.

Homestead Exemption

Up to $50,000 off assessed value on a buyer's primary residence — plus Save Our Homes 3% annual cap. Doesn't affect closing math; affects buyer's tax estimate.

FIRPTA Withholding

15% withholding on sales by foreign sellers, with exemption thresholds at sub-$300K primary-residence sales. Documented and remitted at close.
