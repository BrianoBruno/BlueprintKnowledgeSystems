About Rally Point

A boutique title firm built around one promise.

Every closing should feel calm, clear, and complete. That promise has guided every file we've handled since day one.

RP
Our Founder

Built on relationships, run on detail.

Rally Point Title Agency was founded to bring back the lost art of a personal closing — the kind where the title officer knows your name, picks up the phone, and walks every party through the file. We serve buyers, sellers, agents, lenders, and investors across NJ, NY, PA, and FL with the same handshake-first standard we opened with.

Who we serve

Real estate agents, attorneys, lenders, buyers, sellers, and investors.

We are a full-service title & escrow agency serving all of New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania & Florida. With offices located in Monmouth County, New Jersey, West Palm Beach, Florida, and Pennsylvania, we focus on delivering personalized, white-glove service through expertise, innovation, and a streamlined closing process.

What makes Rally Point different

Three things every closing gets — every time.

White-glove service

Plain-English answers, a direct line to your settlement officer, and a calm hand at the table. We treat every closing the way we'd want our own family's closing handled.

Wire-fraud zero

Every wire is verified by voice, every account confirmed. We've never lost a dollar to wire fraud and we work hard to keep that record perfect.

Multi-state expertise

Four states, one team. Whether your file sits in Monmouth County, Manhattan, the Lehigh Valley, or West Palm Beach, the people closing it know the local nuance.
